# Change appearance

Change how Phials looks without changing your files, file views, or window arrangement. Appearance settings are saved and reused when you open Phials again.

- [Choose a color scheme and theme](./choose-a-color-scheme-and-theme.md) to follow your system appearance or keep Phials light or dark with a theme for each mode.
- [Use window transparency](./use-window-transparency.md) to switch the main and Settings windows between see-through and opaque chrome.
- [Adjust the interface zoom](./adjust-the-interface-zoom.md) to make the complete interface larger or smaller.
