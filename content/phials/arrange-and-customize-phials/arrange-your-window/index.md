# Arrange your window

Arrange supporting panels around the center, divide the center into tab groups, or keep complete center environments as named **Layouts**. Layouts autosave independently from the global panel arrangement, so switching Layouts never moves your docks.

- [Arrange docks and panels](./arrange-docks-and-panels.md) covers dock visibility and size, panel placement, panel groups, and automatic restoration.
- [Create tab groups and split views](./create-tab-groups-and-split-views.md) covers center-tab placement, local splits, group sizing, and empty groups.
- [Create and switch Layouts](./save-and-reuse-layouts.md) covers autosave, New Layout, duplication, switching, identity, deletion, and the distinction from saved views.
