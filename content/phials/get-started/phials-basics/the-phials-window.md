# The Phials window

The Phials window keeps the files you are working with in the center and supporting tools around the edges. The major surfaces can move, but their roles stay the same.

## The center

The center contains tabs. An **Explorer tab** browses one location and owns its own navigation, selection, and file view. When a file opens in the center, Phials creates or focuses a **file tab** for that file. Other features, such as Web Browser or Terminal, can also appear as center tabs.

A tab belongs to one **tab group**. When the center has more than one tab group, the groups form a **split view**, so several active tabs can remain visible at once.

## Docks and panels

The **left dock**, **right dock**, and **bottom dock** are placement areas for movable **panels**. A panel is a named surface such as **Navigator**, **File**, **Property Browser**, or **Terminal**.

On desktop, the default arrangement puts **Navigator**, **Workspaces**, and **Property Browser** on the left and **File** on the right. The bottom dock starts closed. You can show, hide, resize, or rearrange these areas without changing your files.

The File panel follows what you select in the active Explorer tab. Choose **File** to see a preview and metadata or **Page** to see properties and notes. Open a file to keep working on it in a center tab.
