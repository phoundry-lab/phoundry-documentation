# Phials basics

Phials keeps ordinary files and folders at the center of your work, then adds ways to view, find, describe, and arrange them. These overviews establish the main parts of the application and how they relate. You can read them in order or start with the area you want to understand.

- [The Phials window](./the-phials-window.md) explains the center, docks, tabs, and panels.
- [Browsing in Phials](./browsing-in-phials.md) introduces the Navigator panel, locations, and Explorer tabs.
- [File views and finding files](./file-views-and-finding-files.md) shows how one folder can be presented and narrowed in different ways.
- [Files and Pages](./files-and-pages.md) distinguishes selection-following panels from persistent file tabs, File mode, and Page mode.
- [Organizing with Workspace Folder data](./organizing-with-workspace-folder-data.md) explains the information Phials can store around ordinary files.
- [Arranging your window](./arranging-your-window.md) introduces docks, tab groups, split views, and Layouts.
