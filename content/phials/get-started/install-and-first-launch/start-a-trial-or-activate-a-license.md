# Local use and Remote Access

Phials is free for local use. Open the app and [complete first launch](./complete-first-launch.md). You do not need an Account, license key, activation, trial, or internet connection for local work.

## Sign in for Remote Access

Remote Access connects your personal devices through a verified Phoundry Account. It costs USD $4 per month per Account and covers unlimited personal desktop and iOS devices. There is no annual plan or trial. Supporter is a separate USD $20 one-time purchase that grants a badge only.

1. Open **Settings → This Client → Account** and choose **Create an Account** or **Sign in**.
2. Verify your email using the verification link.
3. On desktop, use **Manage Account** or the subscribe button to start your subscription.
4. Sign in to the same Account on your other devices and choose **Refresh**.

On a subscribed desktop, **Hosting** turns on automatically unless you’ve turned it off before. Under **Settings → This Client → Remote Access**, you can give the device a recognizable name and change **Hosting**. Keep Phials open on that desktop while connecting from another device. On iOS, you can connect to a desktop.

## Find a Host

Open the device menu at the bottom of the Phials window and choose the desktop you want to browse. If it’s missing, choose **Refresh** or open **Add Host…** to check your account and linked desktops. If the desktop is unavailable, check that Phials is open there and **Hosting** is on, then choose **Retry**.

Choose the device marked **Local** to return to the files on the computer you’re using.

## Connectivity and access changes

Phials needs an internet connection to check your account each time you connect or reconnect. If it can’t verify your account when you open Phials, choose **Refresh** in Account settings once you’re online.

Turning off **Hosting** or signing out on the desktop closes its incoming connections immediately. Changes you make on another device take effect when Phials next checks your account.

Cancel renewals through the [Account portal](https://account.phoundry.app/account). Access continues through the paid period. A failed renewal allows seven days beyond the last paid period; repeated retries do not extend this grace. An unpaid initial checkout grants no access.

## Recover an Account

Choose **Forgot password?** and open the recovery email on the device or browser where you requested it. Existing users who signed in with email links can establish a password this way. Standard password recovery signs out other device sessions. You can also revoke device access in the Account portal.
