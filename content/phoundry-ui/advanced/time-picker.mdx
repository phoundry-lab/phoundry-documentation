## Usage tips

- Use `bind:hour` and `bind:minute` to keep your state in sync with the picker.
- The component automatically handles 12-hour to 24-hour conversion internally.
- The `hour` prop is always a 24-hour value (0-23).

## CSS styling

`.ph-time-picker` is the stable identification hook for TimePicker. Part selectors and `--_ph-*` properties are internal. Component CSS is scoped and unlayered; Consumer classes remain additive and utility overrides may lose precedence.

Clock and duration modes keep their value and callback contracts. Field widths and typography are owned by CSS; duration hours remain wider than minute/second fields.

Keep the shared component stylesheet and a named Theme imported. Decorative motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
